# The Shell

The Shell is a mod for neoforge 1.21.1 that adds a new dimension to explore. With multiple biomes filled with a lot of animals and plants.

The conch, found at the bottom of a new jungle temple, will bring you to a paradisiacal archipelago with a rich variety of flora and fauna, be aware of big creatures on and in the sea…

Take you're submarine to explore the abyss. You can find a mysterious crystal 

Full details on items, blocks, creatures and structures are on the wiki.
